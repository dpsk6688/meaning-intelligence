六层元智能栈The Six-Layer Meta-Intelligence Stack

六层按认知距离分为三带：界面带解决「信息进不来」，加工带解决「意义出不来」，元带解决「方向守不住」。只有元带具备改写下层前提的能力——这正是「元」的形式特征。 The six layers fall into three bands by cognitive distance: the interface band solves "information cannot get in", the processing band "meaning cannot get out", and the meta band "direction cannot be held". Only the meta band can rewrite the premises below it.
