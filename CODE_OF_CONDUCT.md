## 我们的承诺 / Our Pledge

**中文**

我们承诺：让参与本项目的体验不因年龄、体型、可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、社会经济状况、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教、性或性取向而受到骚扰。

本项目的讨论对象是理论与证据，不是人。我们承诺把"可检验性"作为评价主张的标准：**可以攻击理论，不可以攻击人。**

**English**

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

What this project discusses is theory and evidence, not people. We pledge to judge claims by testability: **attack the theory, not the person.**

## 行为标准 / Our Standards

**中文 —— 有助于营造良好环境的行为**

- 对他人保持同理心与善意；
- 尊重不同的意见、观点与经验；
- 给出**可复核**的批评：指出文件、行号、卡片编号或数据位置；
- 接受建设性反馈，也愿意更正自己先前的错误；
- 对错误负责，向受影响的人致歉，并从经验中学习；
- 把注意力放在对整个社区与对可检验性最有利的事情上。

**中文 —— 不可接受的行为**

- 性化的语言或图像，以及任何形式的性关注或挑逗；
- 挑衅、侮辱、贬损性评论，人身攻击或政治攻击；
- 公开或私下的骚扰；
- 未经许可公开他人的私人信息（如住址、邮箱、账号）；
- 针对报告人身份、动机、能力或人格的评论，而不是针对其方法与数据；
- 其他在专业场合可被合理认定为不当的行为。

**English — Behavior that builds a good environment**

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people;
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- Giving criticism that can be **checked** — naming the file, line, card identifier, or data location;
- Accepting constructive feedback, and being willing to correct your own earlier errors;
- Taking responsibility, apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and learning from them;
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for testability.

**English — Unacceptable behavior**

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind;
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- Public or private harassment;
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission;
- Commentary on a reporter's identity, motives, competence, or character instead of on their methods and data;
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## 执行责任与适用范围 / Enforcement Responsibilities and Scope

**中文**

维护者负责澄清并执行上述标准，对被认为不当、威胁、冒犯或有害的行为采取适当且公平的纠正措施，并有权删除、编辑或拒绝与本准则不符的评论、提交、代码、issue 及其他贡献，在适当时说明理由。

本准则适用于所有项目空间（包括仓库、issue、pull request、讨论区），也适用于个人在**公开场合**正式代表本项目时的行为。

**English**

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful. They have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons when appropriate.

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, discussions) and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in **public spaces**.

## 报告 / Reporting

**中文**

如遇滥用、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为，请通过以下渠道报告：

- 维护者 GitHub 主页：<https://github.com/sara-protocol>

本项目**不公布邮箱地址**，也不要求报告者提供真实身份。请在报告中包含：发生了什么、发生在哪个链接或 issue、时间，以及你希望得到的处理方式。所有投诉都会被及时、公正地审阅与调查；维护者有义务尊重报告者的隐私与安全。

请注意：GitHub 平台本身也提供屏蔽、举报与内容移除机制，适用于账号级的滥用情形。

**English**

To report abuse, harassment, or other unacceptable behavior, use:

- Maintainer profile on GitHub: <https://github.com/sara-protocol>

This project **publishes no email address** and does not require reporters to reveal their identity. Please include what happened, the link or issue where it happened, when it happened, and the outcome you are seeking. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Note that GitHub itself also provides blocking, reporting, and content-removal mechanisms for account-level abuse.

## 执行指南 / Enforcement Guidelines

**中文 —— 维护者将按后果的严重程度依次采取以下措施**

1. **更正 / Correction**：私下书面警告，说明违规性质，并解释为什么该行为不当。可能要求公开道歉。
2. **警告 / Warning**：警告并说明继续违规的后果。在一段时间内不得与相关人员互动，包括主动联系执行本准则的人。违反这些条件可能导致临时或永久封禁。
3. **临时封禁 / Temporary Ban**：在一段时间内禁止与项目进行任何形式的互动或公开交流。在此期间不得公开或私下联系相关人员。违反可能导致永久封禁。
4. **永久封禁 / Permanent Ban**：永久禁止在项目内进行任何形式的公开互动。

**English — Maintainers will follow these guidelines in determining consequences**

1. **Correction**: A private, written warning clarifying the nature of the violation and explaining why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with the people involved for a specified period, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this Code of Conduct. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary Ban**: A temporary ban from any form of interaction with, or public communication within, the project for a specified period. No public or private interaction with the people involved during this time. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.
4. **Permanent Ban**: A permanent ban from any form of public interaction within the project.
